- `01:30.2` But in our story, you're one of them. Your word is law.

## Chapter 3: The Power to Change Everything (01:40–02:40)

- `01:41.0` So you start with your first royal decree.
- `01:43.7` **YOU:** Free ice cream every Friday!
- `01:46.3` The crowd goes wild. People chant your name in the streets. Your approval rating skyrockets.
- `01:52.5` Then your treasurer shows you the bill. Ice cream for millions of people isn't cheap.
- `01:57.7` Here's the first lesson of power: money doesn't appear out of nowhere. It comes from taxes, and every coin you spend on one thing is a coin you can't spend on something else.
- `02:08.3` Schools or roads? Hospitals or the army? Lower taxes or better services?
- `02:14.4` Every ruler in history has faced these same trade-offs, and none of them got it perfect.
- `02:19.9` Economists call this opportunity cost. Kings just call it a headache.
- `02:24.7` So you swap the ice cream for something smarter: new schools, cleaner water, and roads that don't eat your car.
- `02:31.7` Your approval rating dips. Nobody throws a parade for plumbing.
- `02:36.2` But years from now, it might matter far more.

- `02:41.0` Soon, there's a line outside your throne room that never ends.

- `02:53.2` Foreign ambassadors bring gifts, and every gift comes with a favor attached.

- `03:22.2` History is full of rulers who were surrounded by yes-men, right up until disaster struck.
- `03:28.1` So you make a new rule: anyone who brings you bad news gets a reward, not a punishment.
- `03:34.0` Suddenly, your advisors have a lot more to say, and some of it is actually useful.

- `04:04.8` There's no perfect answer. Every choice helps some people and hurts others.

- `06:51.0` And then you have your boldest idea yet.
- `06:53.7` What if the ruler had to follow the rules too, just like everyone else?

- `07:39.8` It took centuries, but ideas like that helped shape many modern democracies.
- `07:45.1` The strongest thing you can do with power... is put limits on it.
