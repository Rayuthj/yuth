## Description

```
What if you woke up tomorrow and someone knocked on your door to tell you: the country is yours?

In this animated thought experiment, one ordinary stickman is suddenly crowned king. Palaces, gold, an army waiting for orders, and the power to change millions of lives. But every decision has a price, everyone wants something, and the people around you start telling you only what you want to hear. Will you be remembered as a hero... or as a warning?

Along the way: why most modern kings and queens are mostly symbolic, how taxes and budgets actually work, Lord Acton's famous warning about power, Shakespeare's "uneasy lies the head that wears a crown", and the Magna Carta of 1215, when even a king had to accept he was not above the law.

The country in this video is imaginary. The ideas about power, responsibility and limits on rulers are real.
```
